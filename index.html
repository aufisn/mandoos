Help & Guide

This tool is designed to help you capture what matters, focus your attention, get things done, and learn from the decisions you make.
Each item has a clear place
Vault — Capture it
Use the Vault for ideas, questions, observations, opportunities, concerns, or anything worth remembering before you decide what to do with it. A Vault item carries no commitment yet.
Priority — Focus on it
Use Priority for what deserves your limited attention now. A Priority is not simply something important; it is something important enough to take precedence over other competing demands.
Action — Do it
Use Action for a specific piece of work that needs to be completed. Actions have clear ownership and should be concrete enough to be marked done.
Decision — Choose, record why, and learn
Use Decision when you make a meaningful choice under uncertainty. Record what you decided, your reasoning, and your confidence at the time, then compare it later with the actual outcome to improve your judgment.
In simple terms:
Vault = Remember  ·  Priority = Focus  ·  Action = Execute  ·  Decision = Choose & Learn
Together, they create a simple flow from capture → focus → execution or decision → learning.
Today
The screen you land on
Shows pulled Priorities (up to your pull cap) plus anything of any kind due today or overdue, regardless of whether it's pulled. Snoozed items never show here, even past their due date.
Card border color
Tells you the kind at a glance — matches the legend pills at the top: Priority, Action, Decision, Vault, Agenda. No separate label pill needed on the card.
Due-date pill color
Red = due today or overdue. Gray = due later. Shows the actual date (e.g. "due Sep 4"), not a countdown.
Card indicators (due date, impact/urgency, confidence, assignee, tags, focus time)
Always shown in that order, on any card (Today or Log) that has the data. A field with nothing to show is simply skipped — no blank pill, no gap — so whatever's next just moves up.
Focus time pill (⏱ 2h 15m)
Appears on any card — any kind, pulled or not — once it has any cumulative Focus time at all, summed across every Focus session ever linked to it. No pill at all means zero Focus time logged yet.
Clock icon, top-right of a card
Starts a Focus session linked to that item immediately — no "which item?" prompt, unlike the main Focus button. Gray normally, colored on hover. If a Focus session is already running elsewhere, you get a toast instead of it being silently replaced.
Swipe right on a card
Marks it complete — fires instantly with no confirm. Only works if it already has at least one Outcome Log entry (and, for Decisions, non-empty Reasoning). Otherwise the panel simply doesn't appear at all — no color, no message, the card just slides back.
Swipe left on a card
A short pull starts a Focus session linked to that item — always available, but not from the very first pixel: there's a real dim "safe zone" right after the drag begins where releasing does nothing at all, so a barely-there nudge can't accidentally start a timer. Pull past that and it arms (visibly brighter); pull further, past the trigger distance, and it escalates to Delete instead (icon and color both shift, olive clock → red trash) — but only if the item qualifies (zero Outcome Log entries, no open items linked to it). If it doesn't qualify, any pull past the safe zone just starts the timer, at any distance. If a Focus session is already running elsewhere, you get a toast instead of it being silently replaced. Icon and label sit at the edge that reveals first, so what you see always matches what release will actually do. Agenda cards are the one exception — no Timer arm at all; a left swipe on an Agenda only ever offers Delete, once past the trigger distance.
Tap a card
Opens Edit for that item.
Pull down from the top
Pull-to-refresh — recomputes which Priorities are pulled onto Today. Start the drag outside a card so it doesn't get read as a swipe.
Swipe left/right on empty space (not on a card)
Moves between Today, Log and Setup, in that fixed order — swiping left from Setup or right from Today does nothing (it doesn't wrap around). A swipe that starts on a card is always read as a card swipe instead, never a tab change; this gesture is also off while Add/Edit is open.
"→ N items" / "from: ..."
Rollup breadcrumbs — tap "→ N items" to jump to Log filtered to everything linked from this item; "from:" shows what this item was spawned or spun off from.
Focus (⏱)
Tap while idle
Opens Start Focus — pick "General" or search an open item to link, plus optional Category tags (General-type tags, for office-time you want to track without tying it to a task, e.g. Paper Work, Email — add new ones from Setup with %name).
Tap while running
Pauses it — the button shows a double-ring "halo" and a tinted color while paused. Paused time is never counted.
Tap while paused
Resumes it, right where it left off.
Press and hold (~half a second)
Opens Stop & Log, from either running or paused. Add an optional note — if the session was linked to an item, a "Focused Xm — note" entry is appended to that item's Outcome Log automatically.
Log
Kind chips
Colored like their kind, and double as the legend. All five (Priority, Action, Decision, Vault, Agenda) start selected (shows everything) — tap to narrow, tap again to bring back.
Search: plain words
Every word must appear somewhere in the item's text. Multiple words are ANDed — letter mof needs both words present.
Search: @name
Only items assigned to someone whose name contains that text.
Search: #tag
Only items carrying a tag whose name contains that text.
Search: &overdue
Only items past their due date.
Search: &due <phrase>
Only items due on that exact date. Phrase can be today, tomorrow, a weekday name, an ISO date, or a day-first slash date like 20/7/26. Everything stacks: &overdue @sarah #gas ANDs all three.
✕ inside the search box
Clears the search text in one tap instead of backspacing word by word.
Bold/highlighted words in results
Shows exactly why that item matched your search — could be in the text, the assignee, or a tag.
"Select" button
Enters bulk mode: checkboxes appear on cards, swipe is disabled, and the bottom nav is replaced by a bulk bar — Snooze 1d/3d/7d, +Tag, Close, Cancel. Bulk Close only closes items that already qualify (have an outcome logged) and tells you plainly how many it skipped and why.
Completed items
Always sorted below active ones (same kind order: Priority, Action, Decision, Vault), shown dimmed, with swiping turned off — nothing left to do with them.
Add (+)
Only the text is required
Everything else — kind, due date, tags, kind-specific fields — is optional at capture time.
Shape row: Priority / Action / Decision / Agenda / Leave as-is
Permanent once chosen — you can't re-shape it later. "Leave as-is" creates a Vault item, which is the one exception: Vault items alone can be promoted afterward, from Edit. Agenda replaces the rest of the composer with its own Name + Points flow — see the Agenda section below.
Add → Agenda
Type an Agenda Name (free text — a person, a meeting, a theme, anything you want to remember things under) and add one or more Agenda Points. Typing a name that matches an existing active Agenda offers to add your points to it instead of creating a separate one — you can always still choose to create a new one on purpose. Date is optional and under "More details"; there is no Time field.
/P /D /A at the very start of the text
Same as tapping that shape button — live, as you type. Case-insensitive. Deleting the prefix reverts the shape back to unset. But once you manually tap a button yourself, that choice sticks permanently for this Add, even if you keep typing or retyping a prefix.
@name anywhere in the text
Sets the assignee — but only Action items have that field. Typed on anything else, it's dropped from the saved text with a toast telling you exactly why, never silently lost and never force-fit somewhere it doesn't belong. Matches your longest existing tag first (so @ahmed salim finds an existing "Ahmed Salim" tag instead of creating a new "ahmed").
#tag anywhere in the text
Adds that tag — works on every kind, always applied and cleaned out of the saved text.
&due <phrase> / &overdue anywhere in the text
Sets the due date — works on every kind. &overdue sets it to yesterday. If the phrase after &due isn't understood, it's flagged in the live preview and left untouched in the text rather than guessed at or dropped.
The chip row under the text box
A live preview of everything that will be applied (or dropped, or flagged) before you save — nothing happens silently.
Edit (tap any card)
"Move to" row on a Vault item
The one way to change an item's kind after creation. Pick Priority/Action/Decision to promote it — the matching fields appear immediately, exactly like a new entry of that kind. "Leave as Vault" keeps it unshaped.
Decision Reasoning & Confidence
Each locks into read-only the first time it's saved non-empty, so it can't be quietly rewritten once you know the outcome. Reasoning is required (non-empty) before a Decision can close; Confidence stays optional forever.
Outcome Log
An append-only "what happened" trail — add entries any time via "What happened?". Every kind needs at least one entry before it can close. Every Focus session ever linked to the item collapses into one line — "Aug 3 – Aug 15 · 6h 35m focused" — instead of one entry per session, so the log stays readable; nothing is deleted, the individual sessions are still all there underneath.
Snooze & Due date
Snooze (1d/3d/7d/Clear) hides an item from Today no matter its due date, and disappears entirely once the item is closed — meaningless at that point. Due date stays visible on closed items, as history.
Completing an item
Swipe-right on the card, or the "Complete" button inside Edit — same rules either way (outcome logged, reasoning set for Decisions), same History. "Back" in the header always just leaves the editor (it asks first if you have unsaved changes) — it never completes or discards a saved item.
Opening a closed item
Everything is read-only — text, fields, tags, due date. "Save" is replaced by "Reopen", which sets it back to open, logs a factual "Reopened" entry, and immediately unlocks the sheet for normal editing.
Agenda
What an Agenda is
A named context holding several things you want to remember together — not a person record, not a single task. Agenda Points are the individual things; the Agenda Name is just what you're grouping them under.
Agenda Points, inside Edit
Add, cover/uncover (tap the checkbox), Remove, or Convert to a real Priority/Action/Decision/Vault item. All of these stage onto the draft — nothing is written until you tap Save (or Complete Agenda), and Back discards them with the same "Discard unsaved changes?" warning every other edit uses.
Active Agenda lookup
Matches the typed name anywhere, not just from the start — searching "Ahmed" finds "Meeting with Ahmed" too. Selecting a match appends your points to that exact Agenda by ID; there's always a "Create new Agenda" option alongside it if you want a separate one on purpose.
Date — no Time
Agenda scheduling is date-only. An Agenda whose date has passed stays active (never auto-completed) and offers a one-time-per-day nudge to set a new date or clear it.
Completing an Agenda
"Complete Agenda" inside Edit — commits any unsaved draft first, then asks what to do with any still-uncovered points (keep unresolved, move to another Agenda, or convert each to a real item). Covered points are never discarded.
Agenda and Focus
Agenda is not a Focus Timer item — no card clock, no swipe-to-start-Focus. It still locks like every other card while something else is being focused on.
Setup
Three tag types: People, Themes, General
People are assigned to Action items and shown in the People group. Themes are the tags you attach to Priority/Action/Decision/Vault items — the default, and what you get typing a name with no prefix. General exists only for Focus's category tags (office-time tracking like Paper Work or Email), kept deliberately separate so it never clutters item tagging.
Add a tag: @name / #name / %name
@name creates a Person (same as an Action's assignee would). #name creates a Theme — same as typing with no prefix at all. %name creates a General tag, only usable from the Focus category-tag picker.
Tap a tag
Selects it (multi-select) — once anything's selected, ✕ (cancel) and 🗑 (hide/archive all selected) appear next to Add. Hiding a tag never removes it from the items that already carry it — it just stops showing/matching anywhere active. Retype the exact same name later and the same tag comes back, with every old association intact.
Double-tap a tag
Renames it inline. Blocked if the new name collides with an existing tag, so you never end up with two tags with the same name.
Today priority pull cap
How many top-ranked Priorities Today pulls in at once (1–5, default 3). Changing it re-ranks Today immediately and the choice persists.
Appearance
Paper & Graphite or Forest Ledger — switches instantly, no reload, and the choice survives export/import.
Rituals
End-of-Day: one-at-a-time triage over what's on Today — Keep, Snooze, Delete (only if allowed), or tap to open Edit. Weekly Review: a read-only report of the last rolling 7 days — nothing to act on there, just to notice.
Backup
Mandoos lives only in this browser's local storage — Export/Import is the only way to move or protect your data. Import fully replaces everything currently on the device, with a confirm first.
Analysis (Setup)
Chronic Avoidance
Every open item snoozed 2+ times, or a Priority pulled onto Today on 3+ distinct days without closing — everything qualifying at once, not just the one item a live nudge happened to catch.
Decision Judgment Record
Every closed Decision — reasoning as originally locked, confidence at the time, and the actual outcome — side by side so you can judge your own track record honestly. Decisions left open 7+ days appear in a separate "Still open" list.
Focus Neglect
Priorities pulled onto Today 3+ times with zero Focus minutes ever logged against them — things you keep looking at but never actually sit down and work on.
Completion Bias
Average priority score of what you've closed in the last 14 days, compared against the average score of what's currently pulled onto Today — shown plainly either way, never framed as a verdict.
Tap any item to jump straight to editing it
Read-only otherwise — Analysis never changes anything on its own. All thresholds are fixed, matching the same numbers the live Smart nudges use, so the two never disagree about what counts as a pattern.
Smart nudges
Re-snoozing something that's already overdue
If you snooze an item a 2nd time (or more) while it's already past its due date, you'll be asked whether the due date itself needs updating instead — with a shortcut straight to that field. Never blocks: "Snooze anyway" is always right there too.
Pushing a due date later, repeatedly
If a due date has already been pushed later once before, and you push it later again at Save, you're asked if the new date is realistic. "Keep editing" just returns you to the sheet with nothing saved yet; "Save as scheduled" commits it as entered.
A Priority that keeps coming back without finishing
If something has been pulled onto Today on 3+ distinct days without ever closing, you'll be asked if it needs breaking into smaller Actions — on opening the app or pulling to refresh, whichever happens first. Counts real days, not raw refreshes, and asks at most once per day per item either way.
None of these ever block you
Every nudge is a question with two real, equally-easy choices — never a gate. They're read from your own history (Phase 5's event log), fresh each time the pattern is actually true, not a one-time warning that then goes quiet.
Rules worth knowing
Kind is permanent — except Vault
A mis-tapped Priority/Action/Decision has to be deleted and recreated. Vault is the one kind you can promote out of later, from Edit.
Nothing is ever silently dropped
If a typed token doesn't apply (wrong kind for an assignee, an unrecognized date phrase), you're always told — a toast on save, or a warning chip while typing. Never a silent no-op.
Delete vs. Close
Delete means "this shouldn't exist" — only allowed on items with zero real history. Close means "this reached its natural end" — always preserved via Archive, never blocked by open linked children.
Tags are unlimited, on every kind
There's no cap, and it's intentional — the same many-to-many model as labels in any note or task app.
